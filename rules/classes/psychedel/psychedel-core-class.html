Deliriant (1)

Your Dissonance is a pool representing the tightness of your grip on reality. It starts at 0. Upon achieving a rest, your Dissonance is halved.

Your Lapse Threshold is your Psychedel level plus your Grit. It is the maximum your Dissonance may reach, and your Lapse Threshold resets upon completion of a rest.

You know a number of 1st-League Cerebral and Wyrd spells equal to your Psychedel level. You may reselect these upon each level-up. Starting at Psychedel level 8, the spells you know may be 1st or 2nd League.

When you cast a spell this way, you expend Dissonance equal to its League, and reduce your Lapse Threshold by that same amount. You cannot cast a spell if you have insufficient Dissonance, or if your Lapse Threshold is lower than the spell’s League.

When an effect would raise your Dissonance above your Lapse Threshold, your Dissonance is instead set to your Lapse Threshold minus 1, and you Lapse.

When your Periphery is broken, you gain Dissonance equal to half your Lapse Threshold. When you take damage to your Vitality, you may gain 1 Dissonance.

Figment (3)

Turn: You spawn an unmoving, 1-Vitality, size 1 entity, called a Figment, within Grit spaces, gaining 1 Dissonance and reducing your Lapse Threshold by 1.

A Figment appears to be whatever can inhabit that corresponding quantity of space, and can be discovered to be a disturbance in reality with a Mind-Find roll against your current Dissonance. It is dealt 1 card each round. It instantly vanishes when reduced to 0 Vitality, or when you rest.

When your Figment vanishes, you may choose to gain 2 Dissonance.

The following is the attack a Figment possesses, using your stats:

Daydream (4)

Turn: You may spend 1 Dissonance to make up to a singular cubic space not inhabited by any entities instantly and permanently vanish from existence.

Turn: You may spend 1 Dissonance to manifest up to a singular cubic space of any form of material. You must maintain focus on the object by continuing to touch it. It vanishes in its entirety upon no longer being touched.

Contact High (6)

You gain -(the number of entities within a number of spaces equal to your current Dissonance) Defense against Psychic.

All entities within a number of spaces equal to your current Dissonance gain +1 Defense against Psychic.

Turn: An entity you are touching must make a Grit-Resist roll against your Mind-Break or see reality as you perceive it. If you succeed, they take an amount of Psychic damage equal to the difference of successes between the rolls.

Mind Palace (7)

Turn: If your Dissonance is above half your Lapse Threshold, you may reduce your Lapse Threshold by 2 to temporarily override a number of unoccupied equally-vertical spaces equal to your Dissonance, changing them into any of the following, with more up to GM discretion. At the end of the encounter, the effects undo, transforming the spaces back to what they were upon enacting this.

  • Everything within the space vanishes, becoming fully empty air.
  • Everything within the space is filled with a solid material totaling a ton of weight.
  • Everything within the space is filled with a slime-like ooze.

Lucid (9)

When you Lapse, you may add or subtract your Grit from the d100 roll.

Shared Delusion (10)

Your Figments may be any size up to your current Dissonance, and have Vitality equal to their size.

Ego Death (12)

Once per rest, when you would Lapse, you may instead set your Dissonance to 0 and restore your Lapse Threshold to its full value.
